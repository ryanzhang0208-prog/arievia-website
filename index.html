What we do Partner with us

Our mission

To help everyone understand digital money and protect themselves from fraud, through free public education.

How we work

We teach, we don't sell

We don't issue, endorse, or promote any token or digital-asset product. We teach people how to check a product, not which one to buy.

Independent

We don't take money from token projects, exchanges, or stablecoin issuers in return for speaking time or access to our participants. We name every supporter, and no supporter writes or reviews our materials.

Free, and in your language

Our workshops and guides are free, in English and Chinese, and open for libraries, schools, and community groups to reuse.

What we do

  • Teach people to spot scams

    Free workshops and plain-language guides, in English and Chinese, on how crypto and Web3 scams work and how to avoid them.

  • Build a community that looks out for each other

    We're building a network of volunteers, campus groups, and local partners who learn together and help the people around them spot and stop scams.

  • Help founders build safer products

    Our Founder Lab gives student and early-stage teams free workshops on building fraud protection into what they make. No equity, no fees, no token launches.

Our team

Ryan Zhang

Founder and President

Ryan has spent his career on payment and fraud-prevention systems. At Goldman Sachs he was a senior product manager working on fraud and risk systems in the consumer business, and before that, as a consultant with USAA's digital banking and payments teams, he added authentication and transaction controls to wire transfers and phone banking. At Arievia he leads the curriculum and guides, and he teaches in English and Mandarin. He holds a B.S. in Finance from the University of North Carolina at Greensboro and an M.S. in Information Systems from Pace University.

Angie Liu

Co-founder and Chief Marketing Officer

Angie leads Arievia's outreach to partner organizations and participants, runs its English and Chinese channels, and oversees the design of its public materials. She has worked in go-to-market and product marketing at HYPR, an identity assurance company, and in digital experience and growth roles at PVH Corp./Calvin Klein. She holds an M.S. from Columbia University and a B.S. in Psychology and Industrial Relations from the University of Toronto.

Arievia has no paid staff. Everyone on our team volunteers their time.
